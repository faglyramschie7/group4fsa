The current FSA Study presentation has **12 main slides (20-minute speaking budget) plus 6 Appendix slides (`A1`–`A6`, slides 13–18)**. All supporting schedules and calculation tables appear directly in the Appendix slides as well as in the interactive source sidebar (**Explain this slide** or clicking any claim/row). Submitted milestones remain the numerical authority. The latest user instructions set the investor horizon to short term, delay the recommendation until Slide 12, and use factual declarative explanations rather than questions or instructions to the reader.

## What needed improvement

The previous presentation included most required topics, but coverage was stronger than explanation. Ratios, Z-score and liquidity shared one slide. Forecast assumptions and forecast results shared another. Long numerical titles and early SELL / AVOID statements could replace the explanation with a conclusion. The visible slide titles were also hard-coded by position, so simply adding slides would show the wrong title or layout.

The rebuilt presentation uses specific factual headings and separates reported results, their causes or model assumptions, and their implications. The latest edit uses short topic labels, including “Tokopedia Service Fee”, “Revenue” and “Net Loss”. Generic headings, rhetorical questions and instructions to the reader have been removed. The sidebar explains terms in simple language before doing the calculation. It also distinguishes recorded profit from cash, smaller losses from actual profit, available cash from interest coverage, a screening score from bankruptcy, model assumptions from forecasts with certainty, and valuation from trade timing.

## Qualifications that matter

1. **Opening answer versus the latest instruction.** The assignment explicitly requests an answer in one sentence in the opening. The user explicitly requested no conclusion before the end. The revised deck follows the user's instruction: it opens with the company, user and reference price, and places the recommendation on slide 12. This is a deliberate departure from that rubric line, not full literal compliance with it.
2. **Appendix slides and LMS PPTX sync.** All supporting tables are now rendered in **Appendix slides `A1`–`A6` (slides 13–18)** in the web app in addition to the interactive sidebar/pop-out modal. If a static PPTX (`Group4_Presentation_GOTO.pptx`) is submitted to the LMS, ensure the exported PPTX also includes these updated appendix slides and wording.
3. **One forecast (DCF & RI) and same-basis FY2025 multiples cross-check (Updated Guideline compliant).** Under `UPDATED_FSA_Final_Presentation_Guidelines.pdf`, **One forecast** applies to DCF and Residual Income (both retain the Milestone 4 base forecast and `3.0%` terminal growth), while **Market multiples are a cross-check** applied on the same actual basis on both sides without applying trailing peer multiples to forecast figures. The Grab P/B cross-check complies with this updated rule by applying Grab's actual FY2025 parent P/B (`3.0391x`) to GoTo's actual FY2025 parent BVPS (`Rp29.9479`) and clearly stating that basis.
4. **Terminal growth.** Assignment 14 selects 3% manually because the negative-NOPAT fundamental-growth calculation is not meaningful. It is below the assignment risk-free proxy and WACC. This is a bounded analyst assumption, not independent proof of sustainable growth.
5. **Scenarios.** All three sets of scenario drivers are shown. The complete projected statements are the submitted base case. The fixed-cost illustration is identified as a separate cost-behaviour test; it is not relabelled as the fully linked optimistic scenario. No missing scenario outcomes have been invented.
6. **Short-term user.** Financial statements and long-run valuation alone cannot establish the next price move. Catalyst verification, trading liquidity and price behaviour remain unestablished by these assignments. No fabricated short-term target, catalyst or stop-loss is added.
7. **Delivery requirements remain separate.** The guideline also requires all members to speak, a PPTX submitted to the LMS, and Q&A preparation. Removing a speaker-allocation slide does not remove the speaking requirement. This website revision does not itself establish LMS submission or update the existing PPTX file.
