## Corrections and qualifications before reuse

1. **Assignment 8 fair-value error:** 2024 fair-value adjustment loss is **482,134**, not 428,134. Both the 2025 comparative income statement (PDF p. 17) and the 2024 statement support 482,134.
2. **Recurring impairments:** goodwill impairment appears in both 2024 and 2025 (308,114 and 356,830), and fixed/intangible impairment also occurs in both years (24,421 and 217,133). Assignment 8 marks the 2024 fixed/intangible impairment row “No” for recurrence even though the same category appears in 2025. Describe these as recurring valuation risks, rather than automatically non-recurring.
3. **No double-counting fair-value losses:** the 2025 Level 3 other-investment loss of 438,043 is a component of investment valuation movements, not an independent add-back on top of the income-statement net financial-instrument fair-value loss of 596,703. Note 15 explains that the investment fair-value changes are recorded in that income-statement line.
4. **Positive investing cash flow is not operating funding:** 2025 net time-deposit settlements were 1,386,520. Net investing cash flow excluding that line was **(867,403)**. CFO funded only **26.0%** of capex. Explicit proceeds from investment/subsidiary-interest sales were 216,579, or 18.3% of capex; this definition excludes settlements, return of capital and sale advances. Explain those other cash sources separately if discussing total monetization. Deposit withdrawals move existing financial resources; they are not recurring operating earnings.
5. **2024 capex is unusual:** intangible purchases of 6,339,296 dominate the 8.76x capex/D&A ratio. Investigate the transaction-specific asset note before using this as a normal reinvestment rate. The separate 4,006,527 cash release on Tokopedia loss of control is an additional investing outflow, not capex.
6. **Depreciation base:** Assignment 9 already includes a corrective footnote. Beginning net fixed assets also include non-depreciating construction in progress. Excluding beginning CIP gives bases of 940,632 for 2024 and 440,550 for 2025; depreciation/base becomes 23.44% and 45.52%. These are analytical ratios against opening net carrying values, not disclosed useful-life depreciation rates. Cash capex will not equal fixed-asset additions because the roll-forward includes noncash leases and other movements.
7. **Milestone 2 DSO wording:** Assignment 8 gives closing-balance DSO of 10.56 days in 2024 and 10.90 days in 2025. Therefore it rose slightly in 2025; Milestone 2’s statement that it “fell to 10.9 days” needs a stated comparison period or correction.
8. **Finance costs are disclosed:** Milestone 2’s limitation is the workbook input, not the financial statements. The 2025 income statement separately reports finance costs of 535,152 (2024: 494,053), with detail in Note 27b. For interest coverage, select the actual interest component and define it; do not silently equate all finance costs with interest expense. Assignment 6 and Milestone 2 also use different debt definitions (total liabilities versus selected debt). Label them before comparing.
9. **Segment disappearance:** Assignment 8 identifies 2024 “all other segments” third-party revenue of 772,583 and no corresponding 2025 activity. This warrants follow-up to Note 29 and disposal/consolidation disclosures; a dash alone does not prove an undisclosed policy change or misconduct.

## Strongest proposed red flags

**Investment valuation and recoverability.** In 2025, Tokopedia and Bank Jago carrying values were 7,432,326 and 2,894,921: together 10,327,247, or 22.57% of assets. Embedded goodwill was 2,003,559 in total. Other FVTPL investments were 2,328,032, including Level 3 investments of 1,668,574. Repeated impairment and fair-value losses make valuation assumptions important. Distinguish embedded associate goodwill from standalone consolidated goodwill to avoid overlapping reductions. Sources: Notes 14–15 and 34.

**Revenue presentation and recognition judgment.** Note 2s states that GoTo recognizes GOJEK platform service fees when a sale or trip is completed, generally as an agent on a net basis. Principal-versus-agent judgments and customer-incentive classification affect revenue and margins. E-commerce consultancy service fees are recognized over time using TOKO GMV multiplied by an agreed percentage, which requires reliable GMV and contractual measurement. The auditor also identifies on-demand revenue recognition as a key audit matter. These are documented judgment exposures, not evidence of improper recognition. Connect them to Milestone 2 revenue growth and gross-margin improvement.

Related-party revenue of 1,306,110 (7.13% of total revenue), versus 826,455 in 2024, can support this discussion using Notes 25 and 30. Specify the counterparties and terms before drawing conclusions about commercial dependence.

## Illustrative adjustment bridge

Use a pretax bridge so an unsupported tax assumption does not turn this into a claimed adjusted net income. Excluding the selected valuation charges is an analytical scenario; the charges still represent economic losses and may recur.

| Pretax bridge | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Reported loss before tax | (5,275,830) | (1,086,604) |
| Add back goodwill impairment | 308,114 | 356,830 |
| Add back fixed/intangible impairment | 24,421 | 217,133 |
| Add back associate/JV impairment | 126,718 | 0 |
| Add back net financial-instrument fair-value loss | 482,134 | 596,703 |
| Analytical pretax result excluding these charges | (4,334,443) | 84,062 |
| Separately remove share of associate/JV result | 2,305,125 | (249,100) |
| Analytical pretax result also excluding associate/JV results | (2,029,318) | (165,038) |

The small positive 2025 result in the first scenario is sensitive to adjustments and includes associate profits. It is not proof of sustainable operating profitability. Reported operating loss remains 378,254, because these selected valuation charges are below operating profit. Neither bridge is adjusted EBITDA or a cash-flow measure. Recalculate ROA/ROE only after establishing the tax and equity attribution treatment and a consistent asset/equity denominator.

Assignment 9’s 20% haircut to Tokopedia plus Level 3 investments is a separate valuation sensitivity: approximately 1,820,180, or 3.98% of 2025 assets, before tax. Do not describe it as an additional recognized impairment or add it back to earnings.
